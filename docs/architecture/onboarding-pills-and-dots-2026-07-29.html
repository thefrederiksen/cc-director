Pills and dots

Two small things. Here is exactly what each one is.

1The pills

A pill is the little rounded label on the right of every row in the wizard. There is one component, and it currently says fifteen different things:

ReadyIn listNot found Installing...Not installedDone LaterUnchangedNeeds you Waiting for a gatewayConnectedNone 2 set up1 folder3 folders

The part that actually matters. There are only two colours behind all of that: green and grey. So a tool that failed to install gets the same grey pill as an agent that simply is not installed - one needs you to do something, the other is fine and expected, and they look identical.

And counts are wearing status colours: 3 folders is green, as though having three folders were a state of health.

Proposed: four states, one meaning each

PillMeansReplaces
ReadyIt works. Nothing to do. Ready, In list, Done, Connected, Added
WorkingHappening now. Finishes on its own. Installing..., Checking
Needs youWill not fix itself. Always sits next to a button that fixes it. Not installed, Failed
Not set upAbsent and optional. No action implied. Not found, Later, None, Unchanged, Waiting for a gateway
3 foldersNot a pill at all - plain grey text. 2 set up, 1 folder, 3 folders, 9 ready

The rule worth keeping: a pill says what state a row is in, never how many of something there are - and red only ever appears where there is a button to press. That is what stops the Tools screen crying wolf.

2The dots

The row of small dots at the top of every wizard screen. Today they are decoration - they show position and nothing else, and you cannot click them.

Today

Which step is that? How far away is the one you came for? No way to tell, and clicking does nothing.

Proposed - first run

Step 4 of 7 - Tools

Named. Still not clickable: a first-timer should walk the path, because skipping to a step whose answer depends on an earlier one is how you end up half configured.

Proposed - re-run from Settings

Step 4 of 7 - Tools

Clickable. You opened the wizard to change one thing - click its dot and go straight there instead of pressing Continue five times.

Why only on a re-run. On a re-run everything is already set up, so there is no order to protect - you are editing, not configuring. On a first run there is.
