# Fixing the final inspection - the nine findings, what changed, and what is proven

**Written:** 2026-09-05, by the Manager seated to fix the final independent inspection
(`inspection-final.md`: FAIL, five blockers, three majors), plus ruling R20, which the Architect added to
the brief while the fixes were under way. Product worktree `D:/ReposFred/devthrottle-throttle`, internal
worktree `D:/ReposFred/devthrottle_internal-throttle`, both on branch `mission/clean-up-your-throttle`.

Each finding's "Required for acceptance" paragraph was the specification. Every fix below was watched
red by a mutation that restored the old behaviour, then green again with the fix restored; the mutations
are listed under each finding because a test that has never been seen failing is decoration.

## F-01 (blocker) - the library owns the headline; both consumers render it

**What changed.** `ThrottleFigureDto` carries a `Headline` block computed ONCE in
`ThrottleDefinition.Headline`: the denominator, `HasData`, and for voice, typed, phone and every surface
the turns, the fraction and the whole-number percent (rounded half up, once). With nothing counted every
share and percent is null - the empty state is the library's ruling, not a consumer's guess. The pages
(`client-core` `summarizeThrottle`, the Cockpit `HeroRing` and split bar, the phone `MetricRing` and split
bar) print those fields and divide nothing; the browser bucket normalizer no longer maps unknown tokens to
typed or unknown surfaces to "unknown" - it refuses, as it refuses an answer without the headline. The
mentor report's adapter (`throttle.py` `check_answer`) requires and keeps the headline; `metrics.py`
`throttle_values` takes every share and per-surface count from it; the report's rings and the two usage
bars print the headline's percent field (`render_report.py` `Ring.percent`, `Bar.percent`, `bar_pct`);
`verify_throttle.py` holds the page to the percent fields, not to its own rounding.

**The contract test the inspector asked for.** `tools/throttle-conformance/contract/` holds six hostile
wire objects with the answer a correct consumer renders (generated by `make_fixtures.py`, digests in
`manifest.json`). The headline in each disagrees with the counts and buckets on purpose:

| fixture | what a recomputing consumer prints | what both must print |
|---|---|---|
| the headline is rendered, not the counts | 80% and 100% (from counts or buckets) | 57% and 14% |
| the percent field is printed, not re-rounded | 57 (rounding the share) | 61 |
| the empty state is the library's ruling | 80% (from counts) | no number at all |
| no headline | a rebuilt 57% | refused |
| a surface the consumers do not know | folded into "unknown" | refused |
| a modality token the consumers do not know (`spoken`) | typed | refused |

The same files run through: `statsClient.contract.test.ts` (the real `getThrottle` over a stubbed fetch),
`YourThrottleView.test.tsx` and `YourThrottle.test.tsx` (the REAL Cockpit and phone pages rendered, the
ring text read off the DOM), and the mentor's `test_throttle_contract.py` (the real `check_answer`, the
real metrics run, the real `render_report` page, the ring row read back with the verifier's own regular
expression). `tools/throttle-conformance/contract/run_contract.py` copies the fixtures into ONE temporary
directory and runs all four suites over it through `THROTTLE_CONTRACT_DIR`; it passed. The mentor's copy
of the directory pins the manifest's digest, so a copy that drifts is a red.

**Mutations watched red.** Browser: `voice` changed to `spoken` in the bucket normalizer - the exact
mutation the inspector ran against 26 green tests - fails 5 of 8 contract tests; `voicePercent`
recomputed from the counts fails 3 of 8 and 2 of 9 on the rendered Cockpit page. Report: the ring printing
`pct(ring.share)` again fails the re-rounding fixture; the adapter recomputing the percent from the counts
fails 3 of 8. Library: the C# headline tests cover half-up rounding at exactly 0.5 per cent, the empty
state, the surface order and labels, and a refused unknown surface; `ThrottleFeedReadsTheLedgerTests`
asserts the headline is served on hosted `GET /stats/data`.

## F-02 (blocker) - the sender itself requires the throttle proof

`verify_throttle.py` now writes `throttle-verified.json` beside the report when it passes: the size and
digest of `metrics.json`, the page and both email parts; the digest of the library's checked answer; the
library's provenance (below); and the two ring numbers. `send_report.py` calls
`require_throttle_proof` before the size gate and the claim: no record, an unreadable record, another
account or week, any bound file that is not the file in hand, a file set that is not the verifier's, a
library whose commit or dll digest differs from the one in hand now, or a dirty checkout at either end -
each refuses with no command, no intent line and no claim. The mutation that removes the requirement
fails 8 sender tests. `run_report.py` still runs the verifier before the send; it is no longer the only
thing standing between drifted metrics and an email.

## F-03 (blocker) - built from source every run, with provenance

`throttle.py` `library_dll` runs `dotnet build` on the project on EVERY call (incremental, seconds when
nothing changed) before running the tool; a failed build exits 1 with the build's output and never runs
the old dll. `throttle.library_provenance` reads, with git at the moment of use, the product checkout's
commit, whether anything under the library's source is uncommitted, and the dll's digest. The verifier
records it and warns on a dirty checkout; the sender re-reads it and refuses a mismatch or a dirty tree.
The product's `conformance.py` builds every run too and prints the provenance in its report. Tests cover
the build-first order with and without a dll present, a failed build, and provenance against a real
throwaway git checkout (clean, dirty under the source, clean elsewhere, a new commit, a rebuilt dll).

## F-04 (blocker) - every window form enforces span AND age

`ThrottleDefinition.WindowRefusal(from, to, now)` is the one policy: ends after it starts, no longer than
retention, does not BEGIN before now minus retention, and has begun. The explicit and week forms of
`StatsPageEndpoint.ResolveWindow` and the library's command line (`Program.cs`) all call it. Boundary
tests sit at exactly retention ago (served), one second inside (served) and one second outside (refused)
for both the explicit and the week forms, plus the inspector's January 2020 probe and a not-yet-begun
window. Removing the age check fails 6 window tests. The old "exactly the retention is fine" control
(1 to 31 August, asked on 5 September) was itself an old-start window; it is now asserted refused.

**The clock-change guard the inspector asked for** (acceptance item 4): Toronto 2026-W44 is pinned at
169 hours (`2026-10-26T04:00Z` to `2026-11-02T05:00Z`) and Adelaide 2026-W40 at 167 hours
(`2026-09-27T14:30Z` to `2026-10-04T13:30Z`). Replacing the next-local-Monday calculation with
`fromUtc.AddDays(7)` - the inspector's mutation that left 37 tests green - fails both.

## F-05 (blocker) - R19 reads a confirmed send, and never expires

`common.attribution_step_date` now carries the sentence on EVERY candidate week from the spanning week
onward until a CONFIRMED send that carried it exists for an earlier candidate: `disclosure_confirmed_sent`
reads that week's `send-log.jsonl` for a line with state `sent`, a provider id and the new
`attribution_step` key. The claim file is never consulted. `render_report.py` records the date it drew
(or null) in `render-verified.json`; `send_report.py` copies it onto the sent line and refuses a render
record that does not say. Tests: the inspector's probe (deploy in W32, first report W35) now carries the
sentence; a claim, an intent with no outcome, and a confirmed send of a page that carried no disclosure
all leave it pending; a confirmed send with it settles every later week; an unreadable log stops the
render. The mutation that reads the claim file again fails 4 tests.

## F-06 (major) - each ledger observer runs on its own

`Session.StampSubmission` walks `OnTurnSubmitted`'s invocation list and guards each subscriber
separately. Tests register a throwing subscriber BEFORE the ledger observer on both the text and the
terminal path (the inspector's probe: expected 1, actual 0), and one where two of four subscribers throw
and all four still run. Restoring the single guarded multicast invoke fails all three. The source pin in
`TerminalTypingIsATurnTests` now asserts the invocation-list walk and the absence of the single invoke.

## F-07 (major) - a spoken claim is reserved, then committed or released

`SpokenClaimRegistry` claims are free, reserved or spent. The prompt route reserves the claim, locates the
session, runs the menu guard and delivers; the Director's `Accepted` commits it, and every other outcome
(a missing session, a menu refusal, a dropped tunnel, a Director failure) releases it in a `finally`. A
reserved claim refuses a concurrent second prompt exactly as a spent one does. Registry unit tests cover
release-then-reserve, commit-then-refuse, 64 concurrent reservations with one winner, commit or release of
an unheld claim throwing, and a reservation in flight surviving the sweep. The route test
`A_spoken_claim_whose_prompt_never_entered_a_session_is_still_spoken_on_the_retry` sends the transcript
under its id to a session nobody has, then to the real session, and reads one voice turn - and a replay
after that is typed.

## F-08 (major) - the field inventory, held on three sides

`tools/throttle-conformance/contract/field-inventory.json` names every path on the library's answer and
which real consumer reads it. `ThrottleFieldInventoryTests` reflects over `ThrottleFigureDto` and requires
the served path set to equal the inventory exactly (a new DTO field with no inventory line is a red);
the browser contract test requires every browser-marked path to survive the real normalizer with the
wire's value, and every leaf of the wire to be inventoried; the mentor's test requires every report-marked
path to survive `check_answer` with the wire's value, and every field the checker keeps to be inventoried.
`conformance.py` now compares the headline (every share and percent), the unit, the window's kind, label
and choices, the retention and earliest instant, the hourly voice and typed values, and repository names
and checkouts; the Python restatement of the predicate is gone - the sentence is read out of
`ThrottleDefinition.cs` at run time. Run live over the owner's 2026-W35: PASS, 57% and 14% against the
independent division's 56.83% and 13.89%; with `--break-predicate`: FAIL on turns, the headline
denominator and the headline percent.

## R20 - the desktop composer knows which of its words were spoken

`SpokenTurnRule` in Core is the one rule (the transcript alone is voice; typed text before or after it, an
earlier dictated segment, or an edit makes the turn typed) and `SpokenTurnRule.Examples` is the table
both surfaces are held to. The Gateway's durable dictation route calls it. On the desktop,
`BackgroundDictationSend` decides the origin by it and hands it to the submit - a mixture is now one typed
turn, matching the phone, where it used to be stamped `DesktopVoice`. `SpokenTurnRule.ComposerProvenance`
sits beside the compose box: every inserted transcript is registered, the box's text-changed hook forgets
one the text no longer holds, and `SendPrompt` stamps what the box says - `DesktopVoice` for one untouched
inserted transcript, `DesktopTyped` otherwise. The false "by construction" comment is gone from
`MainWindow` and from `InputOrigin.DesktopTyped`.

**The consistency test.** `BackgroundDictationSendTests` feeds every row of the table through the real
background Send and reads the stamped origin, and through the compose-box provenance; the Gateway's
`PromptAttributionIsGatewayAuthoritativeTests` feeds every row through the REAL durable dictation route and
reads the ledger. Mutations: stamping every composition `DesktopVoice` again fails the desktop test; the
compose box calling everything typed again fails the compose-box test and two Core tests; the phone
ignoring the earlier segment fails the phone test.

**Not done, said plainly:** a transcript in the box survives a session switch as text but not as
provenance, so a dictation inserted, switched away from, switched back to and then sent counts as typed.
The ledger rows already written are not restated (the mission's standing position).
