  The three worth thinking about alongside DevTiller:

  devrudder — the rudder is what the tiller actually steers. More people know "rudder" than "tiller." Same nautical family, slightly more
  recognisable.

  devbearing — "get your bearings" is an everyday phrase. Means knowing where you are and where you're headed. That maps well to what the product  
  does for a developer with many agents running.

  devthrottle — caution here: in tech, "throttling" usually means limiting something (rate limiting, CPU throttling). Could read as the thing that 
  slows your agents down rather than the thing that controls them.
